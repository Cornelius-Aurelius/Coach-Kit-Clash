BEYOND THE SCORE

Make the game fairer.

Every coach, player, referee and fan deserves to distinguish kits quickly. These are practical awareness examples—not a diagnosis or a simulation of any individual's vision.

◐

Think in contrast

Dark versus light is often easier to separate than two similarly bright shades. Assess kits under the actual match lighting.

▥

Check all five shirts

Home, away, both goalkeepers and officials all need to be distinguishable. Small badges and socks cannot solve a main shirt clash.

✧

Give extra signals

Use strong contrast, clear numbers, consistent patterns and verbal or positional cues. Do not rely on colour alone.

About Coach Kit Clash

Coach Kit Clash is an original free football accessibility puzzle game created and developed by Cornelius Aurelius. Players practise recognising potential kit clashes between two teams, goalkeepers and referees, then select better alternatives. This interactive educational game is part of Cornelius Aurelius's wider Colour Blindness in Football awareness work.

The game is designed for public education and does not provide official match approval, clinical diagnosis, or an accurate depiction of any particular type of colour vision deficiency.

How to play

Look at the shirt colours for both teams, both goalkeepers and the referee. Decide if players could reliably tell them apart.

  1. Choose Clear to play or Kit clash risk.
  2. If you spot the risk, select the best replacement from three options.
  3. Read the explanation after each match-up and select Next.
  4. Earn points for correct calls, build a streak, and complete 18 rounds.

Keyboard: 1 / 2 for judgement, 1–3 for fixes, C for contrast lens, Enter to advance. This lens demonstrates luminance contrast only; it is not a colour-blindness simulation.
